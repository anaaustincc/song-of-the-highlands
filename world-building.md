# World Map and Locations

## Map Concept
A small, connected village map designed for exploration, conversation, and gentle story progression.

## Location 1: Rumah Gadang
The family home and emotional center of the game.
- Conversations with family
- Storytelling
- Family decisions
- Ceremonial preparation

## Location 2: Courtyard / Front Yard
A space for gathering, chores, and casual conversation.
- Small tasks
- Meeting neighbors
- Learning daily routines

## Location 3: Village Path
A connection space between locations.
- Environmental storytelling
- Chance encounters
- Transitions between scenes

## Location 4: Rice Field / Garden
A place for work, rhythm, and seasonal life.
- Farming-related tasks
- Learning about cooperation
- Quiet exploration

## Location 5: Market
A lively social space.
- Trade
- Rumors
- Community interactions
- Learning about outside connections

## Location 6: Community or Religious Gathering Space
Used carefully and respectfully.
- Learning
- Discussion
- Moral teaching
- Shared reflection

## Location 7: Elders’ Space
Could be a veranda, kitchen area, or story corner.
- Oral history
- Family memory
- Mentorship scenes

## Map Design Goal
Every location should reinforce the idea that community life, family memory, and shared responsibility are central to the world.
