# First Playable Prototype Plan

## Goal
Create a 10–15 minute playable experience that introduces the player to the world, characters, and basic idea of matrilineal family life.

## Prototype Features
- Movement
- Interaction button
- Dialogue
- Simple quest tracking
- Item collection
- Scene transitions

## Playable Area
- One house interior
- Front yard / courtyard
- Village path
- Small market or gathering area

## NPCs
- Grandmother
- Mother
- Maternal uncle
- Friend or cousin
- Teacher or storyteller

## Tasks
1. Bring a woven item to a relative
2. Find ingredients for a meal
3. Deliver a message
4. Listen to a story to unlock the next scene

## Flow
1. Start in the family home
2. Talk to an elder
3. Receive a simple task
4. Explore the village to find what you need
5. Return and complete the task
6. Trigger a story scene

## What to Avoid in the First Prototype
- Combat
- Complex branching dialogue
- Large open world
- Many systems at once
- Long cutscenes

## Prototype Success Criteria
The prototype is successful if the player can:
- understand where they are
- talk to characters
- complete a task
- feel the warmth of the world
- learn something about the society through play
