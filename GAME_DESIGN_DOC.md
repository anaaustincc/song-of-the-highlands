# Game Design Document

## Working Title
Song of the Highlands

## Genre
Story-driven exploration game

## Target Audience
Kids and teenagers

## Core Theme
The game shows that patriarchy is not the only possible social structure. It does this by exploring a real matrilineal society in a respectful, human, non-judgmental way.

## Player Fantasy
You are a young person learning how family, community, responsibility, and identity work in a Minangkabau-inspired world.

## Main Character
**Sari**, a curious teenager growing up in a matrilineal household.

## Story Summary
Sari helps prepare for an important family and village event. Along the way, she talks with elders, relatives, and neighbors and learns how matrilineal family life works. She discovers that leadership, inheritance, care, and belonging can be organized differently from what she assumed.

## Gameplay Loop
1. Explore a small village area
2. Talk to characters
3. Complete simple errands or small tasks
4. Learn cultural and family details
5. Unlock new conversations and areas
6. Reach a meaningful story scene

## Core Systems
- Walking and exploration
- Interaction button
- Dialogue system
- Quest/task tracking
- Simple item collection
- Scene progression

## Main Locations
- Rumah gadang: family home and story center
- Courtyard: chores, gatherings, daily life
- Village path: travel and environmental storytelling
- Rice field/garden: work and cooperation
- Market: trade and community interaction
- Community/religious gathering space: learning and reflection
- Elders’ space: stories, guidance, family memory

## Main NPCs
- Grandmother
- Mother
- Maternal uncle
- Friend/cousin
- Teacher/storyteller
- Trader or farmer

## Tone
Warm, curious, reflective, respectful

## What the Game Teaches
- Families can be organized in different ways
- Women and men can have meaningful roles without one being the hero and the other being the villain
- Community and consensus matter
- Tradition can coexist with change
- Culture is lived, not frozen

## Visual Style Idea
Soft, colorful, hand-painted look with:
- lush greens
- warm wood tones
- bright woven patterns
- distinctive curved roofs
- gentle, inviting lighting

## First Prototype Scope
Build a 10–15 minute demo with:
- one small village map
- 4–6 characters
- 2–3 simple tasks
- one meaningful story event
- no combat
